Privacy

Saqviro runs on your computer. Your files never leave it.

  • The app does not upload your files or their contents anywhere.
  • It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no telemetry.
  • It makes no network request on its own, not even at startup.
  • The one exception: Check for updates in Settings asks GitHub for the latest version, only when you press the button. No file name, tool name or identifier is sent.
  • Preferences, favorites, workflows and which tools you use stay on your computer. Saqviro never records which files you worked on.
  • Each job's temporary files are removed when it ends, however it ends.
  • What you type as a password or passphrase is used for that job and then forgotten.
  • The tools on this website run in your browser tab and send nothing anywhere.
